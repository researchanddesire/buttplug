# Complete pack and wiring trial

This is a disposable, source-bound assembly candidate. The pack specification, existing body PCB/J6, routed flange PCB, four rigid parts and master assemblies remain unchanged. `LocalTrayReliefLoomTrial.FCStd` is the latest separate static successor, with actual selected connector bodies, 18 wire Bodies and a native two-notch Tray. `rib-relief-review.json` records the cold readback and exact model delta. The prior `CompletePackLoomTrial.FCStd` is a preserved negative: its two return extensions intersected the old reference-pack ribs. No source or master promotion follows from this trial.

The installed bundle has ten J6↔J7 conductors, two body-side power wires, two requested factory power tails and four separate NTC conductor assemblies. Sixteen conductors enter the neck. GND power lands at J2.2P; both independent sensor returns land separately at J2.2S. No USB data pair runs through the neck.

The selected JST SH10 mates are SHR-10V-S, with SSH-003T-P0.2-H contacts. JST ACHTP-02V-S/SACHP-003G-P0.2 form the body-side pack disconnect; ACHTR-02V-S/SACH-003G-P0.2 are requested on the factory pack. Public dimensioned stepped profiles replace the old rectangular reservations. Undimensioned channels, wall/lance details and contact seating are reconstruction assumptions, not qualified OEM mating/crimp geometry. The exact external geometry and source PDFs are in this folder and `../j7-mate-trial/`.

The loose ACH candidate has centre X−7.5/Y−17.475/Z−1.1, with mating along X. Body wire planes are X−4.1 and pack wire planes X−10.9. Pin 1 is PACK_P at Z−1.7; pin 2 is power return at Z−0.5. This is a reviewable alternative to the old long-Z disconnect, not approval to move a source pack or connector.

The requested power exits stay at (7,−17.7,7.5)/(7,−18.9,7.5). Their formed free paths are about 42 mm, substantially longer than the old 8.283 mm request. The candidate initially consumes the 0.25 mm lateral growth allowance at the exit; that exact growth-envelope intersection is an unresolved supplier exit/growth-port requirement, not a collision waiver or proof of swelling clearance. The nominal pack must not intersect the wires.

Two dry Vishay NTCLE413E2103F524A heads are proposed at X12.5..22.5, Y−13.7, Z±4. This moves their attachment positions 4.5 mm left on the same fixed pack face. The maximum 3×10 mm head profiles are primary-drawing reconstructions. The factory lead OD is informational, not a guaranteed maximum: incoming OD≤1.10 mm is a project acceptance requirement. Factory leads remain straight for at least 4 mm, then use ≥5.5 mm radius. Four thinner extensions cross the 0.95 mm pack/stub passage; factory PVC and solder sleeves stay outside it.

Four TE RNF-100-1/16-BK-SP sleeves (TE 5052894108) are manually cut to 5.0±0.1 mm, then formed around the candidate joints. Intended solder lap is 2.0±0.1 mm, entirely inside a straight ±1 mm central region. Require ≥1.25 mm measured insulation coverage at both ends and installed OD≤2.2 mm after recovery. Sleeves follow the adjacent wire bends; the native solid is an installed clearance limit, not a solder-strength or recovery simulation. Use the published 0..−5% longitudinal change only as a screening bound; inspect actual coverage. The internal joint metal is not released by the wire-body model.

## Stock and physical trial

`parts-and-cut-stock.json` lists exact selected stock and starting blanks. Free-path lengths exclude crimp take-up and manufacturing allowances. They are not final cut lengths. Accu-Glass 100690 is 28 AWG/7 strands, nominal OD0.508 mm, published maximum resistance0.244 Ω/m at20°C and catalog minimum bend3.302 mm. Body ACH leads additionally need incoming OD≥0.50 mm to meet JST. Accu-Glass113094 is32 AWG/7 strands, nominal OD0.4953 mm, maximum0.558 Ω/m and minimum bend1.651 mm. Current ratings in this UHV catalog are maximum20°C test values; enclosed-product thermal/ampacity qualification is still required.

Use an inert pack/body coupon before live electronics. Form and identify each conductor, solder and insulate the four sensor transitions off the pack, verify continuity/polarity and inspect lap/coverage, then fit heads to a representative wrapper. Vishay's Cu-lead iron-solder limit is340°C/30 W/2 s with ≥6 mm distance from the coated head. RNF-100 needs at least121°C to recover; measure the sensor body so it stays within Vishay's allowed process temperature/time. Do not infer this from hot-air temperature. No hard potting, direct overmold, liquid exposure or ordinary crimping of the overall-tinned E2 leads is credited.

The proposed attachment stock is3M5413, 6.35 mm wide,0.069 mm typical total thickness,3M ID7000050064. Start with two6.35×20 mm head strips and two3.175×14 mm factory-tail strips cut from the same roll. They are trial blanks, not released installed contours. Use conformal placement with no imposed sensor compression or stretched-tape preload; keep adhesive off stripped joints and crimp interfaces. Actual wrapper adhesion, head motion, thermal response and aging need testing. Head/tail tape contours and a positive body-side wire restraint are not yet implemented in the saved complete-wire native. Do not credit the connector crimps, J2 solder pads, sensor bodies or foam friction as strain relief.

The earlier bare-mate study proved a 3.25 mm disengagement followed by8 mm normal withdrawal for the housings and thin tool tips. It did not prove the attached wire motion. The closed static routes in this trial do not establish that service path or the complete cap-threading sequence. Conduct unpowered preclosure mating/withdrawal trials using full stock blanks; measure handling forces and wire curvature, then select a restrained final form before trimming. No hand force should reach a sensor head or solder pad. The first added circular service loops were rejected after wire-crossing checks and remain archived as failed studies; they are not installed parts.

`archive/rejected-factory-bends/` preserves the first complete native and localized actual Tray/metal intersections. Its pair pass was stopped after concrete failures; it is not a passing report. `archive/rejected-overlapping-loops/` preserves the earlier curve-only negative candidate. Current checks must name their exact native/path hashes.
