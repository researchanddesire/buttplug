# Contact post for an inert seal trial

Request one initial sample plus spares. This is an **unpowered mechanical/leak
coupon**, not the finished electrical/body-contact material or plating release.
No RFQ has been submitted. Use unplated316L stainless steel for this trial;
provide material identity and inspect all critical dimensions after finishing.

The matching `ContactPost.step` is in coupon coordinates: axis+Y, centerX/Z0,
plastic bearing planeY0, wet faceY−2.3. The native dock's corresponding GND post
is translated(+38,+24.5,−11.5)mm to obtain this file.

The STEP's stem is a **basic thread-root envelope**. Machine and gauge the
M2 thread from the callout; do not turn the stem to that root diameter and
deliver it unthreaded. Discuss the tiny face-grooving tool, remaining bearing
land, concentricity, lead/relief, finish and nut access before accepting the
quote. The trial ring is nominal2mm ID×0.5mm CS; the counterface bore is
Ø2.10+0.03/−0.00. Ring tolerance, nut torque/locking, plastic creep, pullout and
casting compatibility are experimentally qualified interfaces.

Finished powered posts will need separately selected electrical contact and
termination finishes, with dimensions specified after plating/polishing and
finished body-contact suitability reviewed. Passing this inert coupon does
not release those finishes or the complete wet device.
