# Shared dock main PCB candidate

`dock-main.kicad_pcb` combines the 48 source-power, 44 controller and 58 safety electrical references on one four-layer, 1.00 mm board, with six mechanical holes. The original leaf circuits remain the schematic authority. The frozen routed board passes full native KiCad10.0.8 DRC with zero violations and zero unconnected items. Final source-bound checks and the saved-copper resistance report establish the digital candidate; this folder is not a fabrication release.

The fabrication quote candidate requests finished outer copper ≥35 µm, inner copper ≥30 µm, finished via bore ≥0.15 mm and via-wall plating ≥25 µm. Nominal “1 oz” labels do not establish these minima; supplier acceptance remains false. In1 is reserved for continuous ground; In2 contains separated power distribution and low-speed routes. F.Cu/B.Cu carry the remaining signals. Ordinary trace/clearance minima are 0.15/0.15 mm, with 0.50/0.20 mm through vias. The global hole clearance is 0.20 mm. `dock-main.kicad_dru` permits 0.15 mm only between J101-owned features to preserve the USB4105 manufacturer pattern, whose actual spacing is about 0.1751 mm; fabricator acceptance remains required.

The [bench quote packages](assembly-quote/README.md) bind the routed Main
to the matching 3.65 Ω UV boards and include bare-board and assembly options.

The source block's 192 copper objects are retained, along with all 207 main critical objects. Of 59 subsequently authored fanout objects, one graph-redundant one-layer via was retired; the remaining 58 retain their exact geometry and identities. `critical-routing.json` records the authored clock, local bypass, ADC and cut-switch power connections. The approved U615/H706 adjustment is bounded by `routing/u615-bounded-exception.json`. Short residual pad escapes and three ground stitches are recorded separately. `check_main.py` checks the saved native pad/net map, protected copper, screw-head clearances and complete native DRC without ignored checks or exclusions. It can explicitly refresh a regenerated schematic XML binding only after matching all 150 native references and every physical electrical pad.

The frozen `dock-main-MCAD-placement.kicad_pcb` is the StepUp input for parallel mechanical integration. It is a placement/model snapshot, not the final routed fabrication board. `mcad-placement-handoff.json`, `mcad-model-coverage.json` and `mcad-model-checks.json` bind its 138 physical package models and 12 copper-only positions. `check_mcad_geometry.py` compares the working board's outline, holes, component poses, pads, model transforms and actual model-file hashes against that snapshot.

The frame is `dockX = pcbX - 100`, `dockY = 200 - pcbY`, F.Cu toward dock +Z. Board bottom/top are Z5.4/6.4 mm. Bounds are X−48…48, Y−4…68 mm; the native bounding-box centre (100,168) maps to dock (0,32). The four original Ø2.3 mm holes remain at (−8,5.5), (8,5.5), (−8,23.5), (8,23.5); rear holes are (−44,64) and (44,55.7). Each retains an R3 surface copper/via hardware clearance. U615 is at (42,49.5); both selected horizontal GH header positions remain unchanged.

All package STEP files are local under `DockMain.3dshapes/`. They are attributed KiCad package-family models, four primary-dimensional TI package reconstructions and a selected USB4105 1.20 mm stake derivative. The custom parts have editable FreeCAD multi-body sources. They are not exact OEM CAD or received-part qualification. Sources, dimensions, omissions and licenses are recorded in `model-plan.json` and the model provenance files. The USB face remains at X0/Y−5 with its seating plane at Z6.4. Selected nominal stakes reach Z5.2; the manufacturer's ±0.15 mm stake tolerance still needs physical clearance qualification. The old Z5.1 datum is the enclosure window bottom.

The adopted power candidate uses a 4.10 V minimum driver input and a 50 mΩ hot/aged common PCB supply-plus-return allowance. `power-study/` contains native filled-copper masks, actual neck/via calculations and the independent DC tradeoff. `MFG-SPEC.md` and `build-constraints.json` state the requested finished minima and explicit supplier review of the native 0.10 mm J101 annular feature / bounded 0.15 mm hole-clearance pattern. The fabricator must confirm the dielectric build and process limits. The inner layers must not silently use 0.5 oz copper. Shared copper is charged at full source current, including device/UI/contact loops. The 70% efficiency assumption, current limits, startup/inrush, USB suspend/source changes and all hot/aged path limits require hardware measurements. Optical leakage, temperature, material aging and cleaning efficacy remain separate validation gates; no sterilization or powered result is claimed.

`build_main.py` is a guarded placement builder and refuses to overwrite routed work. Do not restart placement to refresh a report. `route_main.py` exchanges only supported wire/via records; its residual mode retains all completed native nets and every protected item. `fill_main.py` clears stale fill caches before rebuilding connectivity and asserts that refill preserves every authored copper net. The generic automatic stub-cleanup loop was stopped by approval review. The final single-segment removal used independent owner/root/peer terminal-path evidence, a fixed input SHA/UUID, and assertions that every other copper signature and zone identity remained intact. Future stub mutations require exact reviewed UUIDs and input SHA. Historical router checkpoints and preflight reports are development evidence, not acceptance reports. Only a current, source-bound final `native-checks.json` can establish complete digital routing checks.
