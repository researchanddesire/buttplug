### Motor bridge option with interlocked nSLEEP

- Drive pin 7 from `MOTOR_ARM && DOCK_PRESENT_N` through a 3V3 logic gate such as SN74LVC1G08DBVR. Preserve IN1 from U4's `PWM && MOTOR_ARM && DOCK_PRESENT_N` and IN2 at ground. Both sleep parts use Hi-Z on nSLEEP low. While awake, IN1 low coasts and IN1 high drives forward. Feeding nSLEEP from the PWM-gated output would repeat the wake delay every pulse. A common layout for the original DRV8212 and nSLEEP variants would need exclusive pin-7 links and a population continuity test. The original DRV8212 requires pin 7 at ground for PWM. [TI gate](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g08.pdf) · [Original motor sheet](../one-board/sheets/02-motor.kicad_sch)
- DRV8212PDSGR retains 0.28 Ω typical bridge resistance and a 4 A minimum OCP trip threshold. At 0.45 A these imply approximately 0.126 V and 57 mW from the bridge at full drive, before trace, connector and winding losses at the real temperature. OCP deglitch and retry are 4.2 µs and 1.7 ms typical. The explicit wake delay is 100 µs maximum, with 2 µs typical sleep entry. Holding nSLEEP high keeps it active through PWM-off intervals. Datasheet sleep currents at the stated conditions are at most 82 nA VM and 2.5 nA VCC. VM may reach 0 V with stable VCC. Use the recommended 1.65–5.5 V VCC range, 0–11 V VM range and at most 100 kHz PWM. Do not infer the startup of the separate motor converter from these chip limits. [DRV8212P sections 6–8](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8212p.pdf)
- Stocked TI DRV8837DSGR is the same DSG land and same nSLEEP/IN-IN pin map. Rated motor peak is 1.8 A. OCP is 1.9–3.5 A, with 1 µs typical deglitch and 1 ms typical retry. Bridge resistance is 0.28 Ω typical, 0.33 Ω maximum at specified room-temperature conditions. Wake is 30 µs maximum at the datasheet timing test conditions. It supports VM 0–11 V, VCC 1.8–7 V and −40 to +85 °C ambient. Its datasheet explicitly allows either supply to be applied first and control inputs driven within limits with either supply absent. Verify motor and converter current, rail drop, actual duty/temperature and winding stall limits before crediting the lower peak rating. Published motor running current is 450 mA max. The manufacturer curve's approximately 0.71 A sample stall is a screening reference, with no production allowance. [DRV8837 sections 5–7](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8837.pdf) · [NFP drawing and representative motor curve](https://nfpshop.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Encapsulated-Vibration-Motor-NFP-E1015-3.7V.pdf)
- For either nSLEEP candidate, keep PWM low before asserting ARM, allow the driver wake interval and measured converter settling, then enable PWM. Keep ARM high across duty updates within one authorized command. Verify default-off behavior on boot, stop, docking, contact bounce, brownout and fault recovery. Measure motor current and VM droop/overshoot at the first pulse and while turning off. The existing implementation clears ARM on every `motor_apply` call and sets PWM before re-arming, so it requires a driver-profile update for this variant. [Existing firmware](../../../../firmware/main/main.c) · [Existing motor bench sequence](../../../design/evt1-bench-ordering/motor-first-power.md)

## Fabrication and assembly

- The four-layer, 1.00 mm ENIG candidate uses both SMT sides. Quote Standard PCBA and have JLCPCB review rails and tooling holes/fiducials on the panel. Confirm outline tolerance against the mating flange and mounts. JLCPCB states ±10% board thickness at 1.00 mm, or 0.90–1.10 mm before any narrower purchase requirement. Approve the actual build-up and USB impedance using the routed layer, adjacent reference plane and fab material. Preserve layer-specific antenna keepout. [Current board](../one-board/compass-one-board.kicad_pcb) · [USB route and stack review](usb-stack-review.md) · [JLCPCB fabrication limits](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/Capabilities) · [JLCPCB assembly limits](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities)
- The seed's 0.15 mm trace/spacing, 0.20 mm drill with Ø0.50 mm via and 0.30 mm copper-to-routed-edge are within the listed 1 oz capability envelope. Audit actual routed inner hole-to-copper clearance, solder-mask webs and paste apertures. Quote filled and capped vias where a via enters a solder land and confirm stencil segmentation and inspection for module, QFN, WSON and LGA grounds. Verify polarity and pin 1 from native Fab and the manufacturers' marks. [JLCPCB fabrication limits](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/Capabilities) · [JLCPCB assembly limits](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities)
- CT1–CT4 remain manual. If Ø1.020 mm is accepted as the nominal finished hole, the vendor's general +0.13/−0.08 mm through-hole tolerance implies 0.940–1.150 mm, with 0.026 mm lower-margin above the Mill-Max minimum Ø0.9144 mm. Obtain the compensated tool diameter and guaranteed finished min/max from CAM before fabrication, then gauge and fixture the contact shoulders against the laminate. Keep CTs, the bare pack lands and JP1 out of SMT placement. ## Pack temperature verification

The candidate native netlist retains TH1 at BQ24074 TS and TH2 at the TPS3700 guard divider. The guard outputs and dock-powered TPS3808 supervisor feed `CHARGE_TEMP_OK`; open JP1 separates that signal from the default-off charge gate. TH3 has a separate J8 connection to ADS1115 AIN2, with a pulsed 10 kΩ divider and shared excitation reference. It provides telemetry. These paths were inspected in the [candidate native XML](../one-board/compass-one-board-native.xml) and [schematic bindings](../one-board/schematic-source-bindings.json). Physical sense returns, off-board conductors and attachments need inspection in the assembled candidate.

The exact pack thermistor was listed at 911 by Mouser Canada and the separate exact tip thermistor at 5,170 by DigiKey Canada on the snapshot date. Plan these as harness/assembly purchases. [Vishay NTCLE413E2103F524A](https://www.mouser.ca/en/ProductDetail/Vishay-BC-Components/NTCLE413E2103F524A?qs=vLWxofP3U2y0E8FFp8KP0w%3D%3D) · [TDK B57540G1103F000](https://www.digikey.ca/en/products/detail/epcos-tdk-electronics/B57540G1103F000/3500367)

1. Leave JP1 open through initial power-up. Commission the charge paths with a current-limited battery simulator. Exercise each NTC independently while the other channels remain valid, including open/short, cold/hot transitions and recovery. Record actual charge current, `CE`, sense voltage, supply droop and response to dock loss/reconnect. Confirm JP1 closure enables both hardware paths to control charging before attaching a pack.
2. Tape the three dry NTCs to controlled, separate pouch sites. Verify support and retention maintain contact without loading the heads, crush or abrasion against metal, or capturing the wires in the cap thread. Test detachment with electrically intact leads. Such a failure can return a plausible temperature and requires installed mechanical qualification.
3. On the test assembly, compare raw readings and the telemetry against independent temperature witnesses at both protective NTCs, the third pouch site, PCM, motor and metal contact regions through motor duty, charging, UV and cooldown. Verify the hardware cutoffs against the finished protected-pack supplier limits, with losses and lag measured and the tip baseline recorded separately.
